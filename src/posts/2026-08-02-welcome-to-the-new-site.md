Hi everybody!

Welcome to the brand-new home of Carey Harwoods on the web! We've given the site a fresh coat of (honey-colored) paint and moved everything to a faster, simpler platform.

Here's what you'll find around the new site:

- **[The Blog](/blog/)** — right here! News, event recaps, and new product announcements.
- **[The Calendar](/calendar/)** — every market and festival we're attending, all in one place. No more digging through old posts to find out where we'll be.
- **[About Us](/about/)** — the story of our little family business.
- **[Patreon Creators](/patreon/)** — the full list of 3D designers we support, whose models we're licensed to print and sell.

Everything else is staying the same: you'll still find us at Summerville Third Thursday most months, and at festivals all around the Lowcountry. Thanks for shopping small and shopping local — we'll see you at the next event!

— Christy
