Carey Harwoods, LLC is a small, family-owned hobby and crafting business based out of
**Summerville, South Carolina**. The shop is run by Christy, with help from
her husband Cody and their kids — a true family operation.

What started as a hobby in 2019 grew into a full-fledged small business in 2022, and
we've been making (and beekeeping) ever since.

## What we do

- **3D printing** — design and manufacturing, from articulated dragons and dragon eggs
  to figurines and custom prints from designers we [support on Patreon](/patreon/).
- **Laser work** — engraving and precision cutting for signs, ornaments, and keepsakes.
- **Fiber arts** — handmade crochet, knitting, and other fiber crafts.
- **Resin crafting** — including resin-based jewelry.
- **Local honey** — raw honey from our own backyard beehives, harvested in small batches
  right here in Summerville.

## Where to find us

We set up our booth at craft fairs and festivals all around the Lowcountry, including:

- **Summerville Third Thursday** — downtown Summerville, every month
- **Flowertown Festival** — Azalea Park and the [Diocese of the Southeast](http://rec-dse.org/), Summerville
- **Sweet Tea Festival** — downtown Summerville
- **Italian Festival** — downtown Summerville
- Plus local markets and other pop-ups around town
